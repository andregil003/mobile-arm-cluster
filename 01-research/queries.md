# Cadenas de búsqueda bibliográfica (Semana 2)

Objetivo: localizar 10 papers primarios (IEEE, ACM, arXiv) sobre Mobile Device Clouds, smartphone clusters y Green ARM Computing.

## Cadenas Boolean sugeridas

### IEEE Xplore / Google Scholar
1. `("smartphone cluster" OR "mobile device cloud" OR "mobile device cloud computing") AND (ARM OR "system on chip" OR SoC) AND ("energy efficiency" OR "FLOPS/Watt" OR "green computing")`
2. `("distributed computing" OR "cluster computing") AND (mobile OR smartphone OR handheld) AND (container OR orchestration OR scheduling)`
3. `"mobile device cloud" AND (latency OR throughput OR Wi-Fi OR "USB-OTG")`

### arXiv
4. `("smartphone cluster" OR "mobile device cloud") AND ARM AND energy`

### Software / virtualización sin root
5. `PRoot OR "user-space Linux" AND Android AND cluster AND scheduling`
